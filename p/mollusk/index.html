How to Play

Welcome to the mollusk. This scenery is your only clue. Find and decipher five six-letter passwords. Then, use them to solve for one final password. Furthermore, guesses must be substantiated. Type your guess and choose the correct word from the provided word map that explains the source of your answer. For example, a password [bubble] with the source [hourglass] is a potential solution. Use the notepad as you wish. Good luck!
